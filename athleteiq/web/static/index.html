AthleteIQ

Train on your own. Get the credit.

Record your drills with your phone. The app counts your reps on the phone itself and sends the numbers to your coach.

Your video never leaves your phone. It is not uploaded, not saved, and not stored anywhere. Only the counts — reps, which hand, how long — are sent. Your coach sees the numbers, never the footage.

Sign in

First time here?

If your coach gave you a short claim code on a slip of paper, enter it here to set up your account.

Parent or guardian?

If a coach sent you an invite code, set up your account here. You will be able to see your athlete's training and choose what they share.

Just your own family?

No club needed. You take the coach's place: you set what they work on, write the messages they get, and see everything. Everything the app does for a team works for a household.

Starting a program?

Set up your club or school here. Pick your sport and the app fits itself around it — positions, the drills it suggests, and which other sports it tells your athletes their training carries over into.

Coaches and directors sign in with the same code and land on the dashboard.
