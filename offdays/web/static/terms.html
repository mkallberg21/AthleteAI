Program Terms

Version 2026-11. For the director who starts a program on 0FFDAYS.

Draft for legal review. These terms describe how the product works today. A lawyer should review them before the app goes to a store or a program is billed.

1. Who agrees

You are the director who starts a program, and you have the authority to agree for that club or organization. When you tick the box at sign-up, you agree to these terms for the program.

2. Your program and its players

  • You will only add players whose parent or guardian has been told about 0FFDAYS and can say yes or no.
  • A parent or guardian signs up first and agrees to training for their child. Nothing starts without them.
  • Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and how it is kept.

3. Fees

The program is billed per rostered athlete per day of the season you set, as shown in the app before any invoice is sent. Nothing is billed until you set season dates and we send an invoice.

4. Use of your program's name and logo on our website

By agreeing, you give 0FFDAYS permission to show your program's name, sport and the logo or badge you upload, on the 0FFDAYS website, in a list of programs that use 0FFDAYS.

  • You confirm that you have the right to share that logo for this purpose.
  • We look first. A new image is not shown publicly until a person at 0FFDAYS has looked at it and approved it. Uploading a different image starts that check again.
  • Only a name, a sport and the image are shown. Never a roster, a player, a count of players, or anything about a child.
  • You can withdraw at any time. In the director dashboard, under your badge, turn off "Show my program on the 0FFDAYS website". It comes off the public list straight away. Removing the badge does the same.
  • This permission is separate from using your logo inside the app, which your coaches, players and parents see.
  • We do not use your logo to suggest that your club endorses 0FFDAYS beyond listing it as a program that uses it.

4a. After you withdraw

We take your program off the list right away. Copies already saved by a visitor's browser or a search engine may take a short while to clear.

5. Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we will show you the new version in the app and note the version number you agreed to. The version you accepted and the time are kept with your program.
