Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Draft for legal review. This describes what the product does today, checked against the code. Before the app goes to a store, a lawyer who handles children’s privacy (COPPA and state law) should read it, and the contact address and company name below must be filled in.

0FFDAYS helps young athletes train on their own between practices. A phone counts their reps, and their coach sees that the work was done. This page says what we collect, why, who sees it, and how a parent controls it.

The short version

  • Video is not uploaded. The camera is used on the phone to count reps. The video is analysed on the phone and is not sent to us or stored.
  • One exception, and only if a parent turns it on. A parent can allow their child to choose a single short clip to send to their coach for feedback. It is off by default, never automatic, deleted after 30 days, and deleted at once if the parent turns it off.
  • A parent is in charge. For athletes, a parent or guardian signs up first, decides whether the child may train, and sends the child their link. Nothing starts until they say yes.
  • No advertising, no selling data, no tracking across other apps or sites.

We do not ask for a child’s home address, school, photo, precise location, or social media accounts.

Who can see it

  • The athlete sees their own results.
  • Their parent or guardian sees their child’s results and everything sent to the child.
  • Their coach and the program’s director see the results of athletes on their teams. They never see video unless a clip was sent as described above.
  • Leaderboards show a name only where a parent has agreed to it.
  • Other programs cannot see anything about your program.

Who else handles the data

  • Our hosting provider, where the service runs and the data is stored.
  • An email provider, only once a program turns on sending. Until then the program sends invitations from its own email.
  • TeamSnap or SportsEngine, only if a director chooses to connect one. We read the team’s roster from it, with the director’s permission. We do not send anything back.

We do not use advertising networks or analytics services that follow people.

Parents’ controls

From the parent’s page, at any time, without contacting us:

  • Say no, or take back a yes, to the child training.
  • Turn clip sharing on or off. Turning it off deletes clips already sent.
  • Download everything held about their child.
  • Delete their child’s data.

How long we keep it

  • Session results: kept while the athlete is in a program, and until deleted.
  • Rep-by-rep timing: pruned after 45 days.
  • Shared clips: deleted after 30 days.
  • Deleted data is removed from the live service; it may remain in encrypted backups until they are replaced.

Children

0FFDAYS is used by children, including children under 13. A child cannot start using it until a parent or guardian has signed up, been told what is collected, and said yes. A coach or director cannot give that permission for a parent. If you believe a child is using 0FFDAYS without a parent’s agreement, contact us and we will remove the account.

Security

Sign-in codes are stored only as one-way hashes. Connections are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, and if something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, we will say so inside the app before it takes effect, and parents will be asked again where the change needs their permission.
