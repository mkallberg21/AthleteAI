What it is

The days between practices, put to work

Most improvement happens away from the field. 0FFDAYS gives your athlete a simple way to train on their own: prop up a phone, do the work, and the phone counts the reps.

Prop it upAny phone or tablet
Do the repsThe phone counts them
See progressStreaks, levels, badges

Your athlete's coach chooses the drills for the team, from wall ball and shooting to push-ups, planks and jump rope. Sessions are short and sized for age.

The coach sees the numbers, never the footage, so they know who is putting in the work and who needs a nudge.

You are in the loop

You see your own athlete's progress, and you get a copy of what the coach says to them, so you can cheer them on at home.

Why it matters

Growth that goes beyond the field

Skills are built in the reps. Character is built in the showing up.

Discipline

A streak rewards showing up one day at a time. The habit lasts longer than the season.

Confidence

Badges, levels and personal bests are earned, so confidence rests on proof of progress.

Ownership

Training alone, by choice, is a skill. Your athlete sets the pace and owns the result.

Resilience

A tough session or a missed day is part of it. Progress is measured over weeks, not one rep.

What you will see

  • Their streak and progress: level, badges and personal bests.
  • What they trained and when, so you know the work is getting done.
  • A copy of what the coach sends them, word for word.
  • Rest days, and a note when the plan suggests easing off.

A small thing that helps

Notice the effort. A quick "I saw you trained today" does more for a young athlete than any score. Ask about their streak, not their score.

Built for families

Your family, your control

Video stays on the phone

It is not uploaded, not saved, not stored anywhere. Only the counts are sent: reps, which hand, how long.

You say yes first

Your consent comes before your athlete starts, and you can take it back whenever you like.

Only your child

You see your own athlete's progress. Nobody else's, and nobody sees yours.

No chat, no strangers

Messages go one way, from the coach to your athlete, and you get a copy of what was said. There is no chat with other kids.

Quick answers

Questions parents ask

What does my athlete need?

A phone or tablet with a camera and a web browser. There is nothing to download.

How long is a session?

Short, and sized for your athlete's age. A few focused minutes beats a long grind.

Is it safe?

Sessions are sized for age, the plan suggests rest after a heavy stretch, and a quick soreness check-in shapes what comes next. It is training guidance, not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and talk to your doctor.

What if we miss a day?

Life happens. A booked absence pauses the streak instead of breaking it, so a family trip never costs your athlete their progress.

Does it replace coaching?

No. It adds practice between practices. Your coach still coaches, and sees the numbers to decide what comes next.
